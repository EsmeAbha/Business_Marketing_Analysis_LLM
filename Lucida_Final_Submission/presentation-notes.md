# Lucida Presentation Notes

## Slide 1 — Problem
Small businesses need a system that can handle research, pricing, customer questions, stock decisions, marketing, and delivery without requiring a full-time operations team.

## Slide 2 — System design
Lucida is a multi-agent AI workforce built with LangGraph. A supervisor routes work to specialist agents and aggregates outcomes into a final answer.

## Slide 3 — Agents
The system includes market research, product vision, pricing, inventory, ad creation, customer engagement, delivery, and reporting.

## Slide 4 — Human-in-the-loop
Risky actions pause for owner approval, enabling safe execution for ad publishing and delivery booking.

## Slide 5 — Memory and observability
The platform stores structured records and semantic recall, and logs every agent event to support debugging, transparency, and cost tracking.

## Slide 6 — Demo outcomes
The system can handle business questions, research, inventory decisions, customer communication, pricing, and final reporting in a single flow.

## Closing message
Lucida demonstrates an operational AI workforce rather than a single chatbot, making it a strong fit for the assignment’s multi-agent architecture requirement.
